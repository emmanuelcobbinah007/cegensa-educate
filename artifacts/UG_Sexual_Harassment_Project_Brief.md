# Project Brief: Sexual Harassment Awareness & Case Management System

## Project Overview
This project involves developing two digital solutions for the University of Ghana, rooted in the official **Sexual Harassment and Misconduct Policy (2017)**.

## 1. Educational Website
**Objective:** Create an "Africa-first," clean, minimalist "organic-tech" styled platform to educate university stakeholders.
- **Key Content:**
    - **Definitions:** Clear distinctions between Sexual Harassment, Sexual Abuse, Sexual Assault, and Sexual Intimidation [cite: 409, 454, 457].
    - **Examples of Misconduct:** Highlighting behaviors like unwelcome advances, inappropriate touching, passing pornographic material, and "quid pro quo" scenarios [cite: 588, 589, 599, 607].
    - **Institutional Framework:** Information on the Anti-Sexual Harassment Committee and CEGENSA [cite: 489, 495].
    - **Rights:** Emphasis on non-retaliation and the right to representation [cite: 561, 567].

## 2. Case Management System (CMS)
**Objective:** A functional system for reporting and tracking sexual harassment cases through their lifecycle.
- **Reporting Workflow:**
    - Support for both oral and written formal complaints [cite: 675, 680].
    - Detailed intake: incident details, dates, places, and names of those involved [cite: 690].
- **Backend/Adjudication Management:**
    - **Respondent Notification:** Automated or manual triggers to notify the accused, allowing 7 days for a response [cite: 692].
    - **Evidence Vault:** Storage for written statements, witness accounts, and digital evidence (emails, WhatsApp, audio-visuals) [cite: 721, 722, 724].
    - **Status Tracking:** Monitoring the 60-working-day investigation window [cite: 747].
- **Security & Privacy:**
    - Strict confidentiality modules to protect the integrity of investigations [cite: 765].
    - Role-based access for the 5-member Adjudication Committee [cite: 528].

## Implementation Constraints
- **Timeline:** Development must be completed by Friday.
- **Primary Tool:** Claude Code.
- **Stakeholders:** Complainants, Respondents, Anti-Sexual Harassment Committee, and University Administration.

## Policy-Based Functionality Checklist
- [ ] **Mediation Trigger:** Option for informal resolution via a mediator if both parties agree [cite: 661, 662].
- [ ] **Sanction Recommendations:** Backend capability to log and recommend sanctions like suspension, dismissal, or formal apologies [cite: 751].
- [ ] **Appeal Tracking:** Log for cases moving to the University of Ghana Appeals Board [cite: 757, 758].
- [ ] **Anti-Retaliation Monitoring:** A mechanism to report and treat retaliation as a new misconduct case [cite: 761, 763].
